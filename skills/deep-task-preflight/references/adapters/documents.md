# Documents, Presentations, and Reports Adapter

Use when the output is read, presented, or judged by an audience.

- Define the audience's decision or understanding as the outcome.
- Require the narrative to answer: what problem exists, why it matters, what was done,
  what changed, what evidence supports it, and what remains open.
- Separate independent workstreams, prototypes, historical exploration, and future work.
- Treat source traceability, factual boundaries, page/section limits, template constraints,
  rendered readability, and result evidence as acceptance criteria when applicable.
- Inspect the rendered artifact from a fresh-reader perspective; do not rely on speaker
  context or the source script to fill missing background.
