# Preflight Challenge Checks

Apply the checks that fit the task before editing. Record any failure in the packet and revise
the packet before the gate.

1. **Stranger**: can someone outside the conversation explain what is changing, why, scope,
   and how completion will be recognized?
2. **Traceability**: does every acceptance ID map to a change/decision and a validation
   action, and does every planned change have a reason?
3. **Boundary**: are adjacent workstreams, prototypes, historical exploration, future work,
   and completed work explicitly separated?
4. **Evidence**: are facts, user statements, measurements, inferences, and open questions
   labeled separately?
5. **Negative space**: what tempting behavior, caller, data, permission, or compatibility
   boundary must remain unchanged?
6. **Failure path**: what partial success, stale state, invalid input, timeout, permission,
   rollback, or boundary case could change the conclusion?
7. **Rework**: would a fresh reviewer still say the background, motivation, scope, or result
   is missing?
8. **Reference parity**: does the proposed path preserve or intentionally change input,
   state, ordering, rendering, errors, and completion semantics of the reference?
9. **End to end**: can each transformation boundary and the final persisted or user-visible
   result be named?
